# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and precise. Welcome newcomers and different
backgrounds. Critique ideas and code rather than people, and allow others to
disagree about symbolic interpretations or implementation choices.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing and
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not
pressure contributors or users to disclose birth details or personal histories.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests and other official project spaces.
The maintainer may ask for corrections, hide or remove harmful content, close
discussions or restrict participation according to severity and recurrence.
Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the relevant conduct when possible.

For routine problems, ask the maintainer for moderation without repeating private
content. A dedicated confidential conduct contact has not yet been designated;
do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue. A private reporting channel is
an outstanding maintainer action documented in docs/GITHUB_SETUP.md.
